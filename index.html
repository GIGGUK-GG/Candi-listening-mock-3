Before you begin

  • The test has 4 sections and 40 questions.
  • You have 40 minutes in total. The timer starts as soon as you click Start and cannot be paused.
  • Each section's audio starts automatically and plays once only. When it ends, the next section opens and its audio starts by itself — you don't need to click anything.
  • You may select text in the questions to highlight it, the same as on the real test paper.
  • You can move between sections at any time, but you cannot replay audio you've already played.
  • After you submit, you'll see your score and the correct answers.

Make sure your sound is on before you start — the audio cannot be replayed once played.
